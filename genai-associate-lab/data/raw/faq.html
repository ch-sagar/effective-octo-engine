Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in the region you selected at workspace creation. Nimbus Cloud supports us-east, eu-west and ap-south regions.

How long are logs retained?

Request logs are retained for 7 days on Free, 30 days on Pro and 365 days on Enterprise.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Use the export endpoint /v1/export to download your data as Parquet. Exports are free and not rate limited beyond your plan limit.

Do you offer an SLA?

Enterprise plans include a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. Pro plans have a 99.9% target without financial credits. Free has no SLA.
